# DPIA outline (to be completed by the data controller)

This is a structure, not a completed assessment. It must be filled in and signed off by the controller, ideally with the DPO or legal counsel.

1. **Description of processing** — purposes (organising the pilgrimage, care of sick participants, safety), data categories (see A, "Data categories discovered"), data subjects (pilgrims, sick pilgrims, volunteers, medical staff), recipients (organisers, staff, medical team, processors), retention (F §9), systems (Supabase EU, Vercel, Expo push).
2. **Necessity and proportionality** — lawful bases (F §10), minimisation decisions (F §6), information to data subjects (privacy notice on the application form and in the app), rights handling.
3. **Risks to data subjects** — for each: likelihood, severity.
   - Disclosure of health status (illness, mobility) to unauthorised people
   - Disclosure of religious affiliation
   - Loss of availability during the pilgrimage (care depends on information)
   - Inaccurate data leading to wrong care
   - Excessive access by volunteers
4. **Measures** — map each risk to controls in F §§2–9 and the hardening checklist F §11.
5. **Residual risk and sign-off** — controller decision; consult ÚOOÚ if residual risk is high.
6. **Review** — before each edition, and after any change to the medical module.
